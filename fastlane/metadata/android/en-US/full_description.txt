<p>Qetara shares files and messages between Android devices and a computer on the same local network, without an account. Android devices can also connect through Wi-Fi Direct.</p>

<ul>
<li>Select several files and track, cancel or retry transfers.</li>
<li>Open, share and find received files in your downloads.</li>
<li>Send files to Qetara from Android's share menu.</li>
<li>Use a session code and PIN for regular sharing and messages.</li>
<li>Enable Flash for nearby file sharing with a verification code and confirmation on both devices. Flash starts disabled and expires after 30 minutes.</li>
<li>Choose a light or dark appearance and adjust the reading size.</li>
</ul>

<p>File contents and messages travel through an encrypted connection between devices. Compare the verification information with the intended recipient. Discovery depends on the local network; some networks isolate devices or block discovery.</p>

<p>Sharing with a computer requires the Qetara desktop application. Wi-Fi Direct is available between supported Android devices. Qetara does not provide cloud storage or transfers to devices outside your local connection.</p>
